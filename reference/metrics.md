# Full Metric List & Formulas

Compute these for every year available (5-10y ideally) so trends, not just snapshots, are visible.

## Growth
- Revenue CAGR = (Revenue_end / Revenue_start)^(1/n) − 1
- YoY revenue growth per year (look for acceleration/deceleration)
- EPS CAGR and YoY EPS growth (diluted EPS preferred — catches dilution)
- Book value per share growth (Buffett's favorite long-run scorecard)

## Profitability
- Gross margin = Gross profit / Revenue
- Operating margin = Operating income / Revenue
- Net margin = Net income / Revenue
- ROE = Net income / Average shareholders' equity
- ROA = Net income / Average total assets
- ROIC = NOPAT / Invested capital, where NOPAT = Operating income × (1 − tax rate), Invested capital = Total debt + Equity − Cash
- Compare ROIC to WACC (rough proxy: is ROIC durably above ~10-12%?) — the core "does this business create value" test

## Balance sheet / financial health
- Current ratio = Current assets / Current liabilities
- Quick ratio = (Current assets − Inventory) / Current liabilities
- Debt/Equity = Total debt / Shareholders' equity
- Net debt/EBITDA = (Total debt − Cash) / EBITDA
- Interest coverage = EBIT / Interest expense
- Working capital trend = Current assets − Current liabilities, over time

## Cash flow & earnings quality
- CFO vs Net income ratio — should be ≥1 over time; a widening gap (net income growing faster than CFO) is a red flag for aggressive accounting
- FCF = CFO − Capex
- FCF margin = FCF / Revenue
- FCF yield = FCF / Market cap
- Capex intensity = Capex / CFO (rising = more capital-hungry business)
- Owner earnings (Buffett's term) ≈ Net income + D&A − maintenance capex − working capital changes (approximate maintenance capex if not disclosed separately)

## Valuation
- P/E = Price / EPS (trailing and forward)
- PEG = P/E ÷ expected EPS growth rate (roughly 1.0 or below is Lynch's "fairly priced growth" heuristic; varies by industry/rate environment)
- P/B = Price / Book value per share
- P/S = Market cap / Revenue
- EV/EBITDA = (Market cap + Debt − Cash) / EBITDA
- P/FCF = Market cap / FCF
- Dividend yield = Annual dividend / Price
- Payout ratio = Dividends / Net income (or Dividends / FCF — the stricter test)

## Share count & capital allocation
- Diluted share count trend over time — shrinking (buybacks) vs growing (dilution, stock comp, M&A funded by shares)
- Buyback yield = Net buybacks / Market cap
- Return on invested capital from M&A (qualitative: did past acquisitions actually grow earnings per share?)

## Ownership signals (when available)
- Insider ownership % and any recent insider buying/selling
- Institutional ownership % — Lynch liked low institutional ownership as a sign of an "undiscovered" stock, though this matters less for large caps

## How to present the trend, not just the number
For every metric above, show at minimum: latest value, 5-year average, and direction (improving/flat/deteriorating). A single-year snapshot without trend context is not enough for this skill's output.
